# Craig privacy

Craig runs on your computer, inside your browser.

- **Listing text never leaves your browser.** Titles, descriptions, prices,
  and contact details are scored locally by Craig's built-in engine.
- **Photo checks are optional and use your own Google key.** When you add a
  Google Cloud Vision API key, Craig sends the public addresses of a listing's
  photos (from `images.craigslist.org`) to Google Cloud Vision, under your
  Google account and Google's terms. Nothing is sent to the Craig authors.
- **What Craig stores, and where.** Your Vision key, your settings, your
  rules, a count of photo checks used this month, and Google's responses for
  photos checked in the last 24 hours. All of it is kept in this browser
  profile's extension storage. Uninstalling Craig deletes it.
- **No analytics, no accounts, no remote code.**
